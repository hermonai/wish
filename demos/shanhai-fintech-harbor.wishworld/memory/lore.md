# Shan Hai Fintech Harbor — Lore

The Shan Hai Fintech Harbor sits at the mouth of three rivers, where myth
and trade have always converged. Its merchants teach. Its bankers
notarize. Its bells ring when a credit covenant is honored.

## Dragon Temple

A sacred place at the city's center. Pilgrims and merchants alike rest
here before crossing the harbor. Lit by candle and oil lamp, with stone
worn smooth by centuries of feet, the temple anchors three quests and
holds the harbor's central ledger stone.

## Merchant Liu

A wandering stablecoin teacher. Liu carries a chest of silver and a
chest of jade tokens. He demonstrates 1:1 redemption, slippage, and the
limits of trust by trade.

## Banker Sun

The harbor's most patient lender. Sun underwrites caravans, oversees
collateral, and explains why a credit covenant is a story you must keep
telling.
